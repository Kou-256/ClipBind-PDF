# Surface: homepage (site/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: everyday Windows users (students, office workers) who copied several images and need one PDF. Action: download the installer (GitHub Releases v1.0.0). Proof on hand: app screenshots (docs/), real feature list, free, offline. No users, counts, or testimonials exist; none may be invented. Bilingual JA/EN, follows browser language with a toggle; light/dark from the OS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one konbini thermal receipt that itemizes your clipboard history and totals to "1 PDF". It refuses the split hero with screenshot plus three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Thermal paper (#fbfaf6) on a counter ground (light laminate gray / night slate). Near-black thermal ink, gray secondary ink, one spot colour: thermal-print red, only for the TOTAL and the download action. Dot-matrix display type (DotGothic16), BIZ UDGothic body, dotted leaders, perforated tear edges, a barcode foot.

STORY: The visitor sees their own habit (copying screenshots) printed as line items, understands that Win+V history is pulled in all at once, sees it total to one PDF, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred receipt about 520px wide, top edge emerging from a printer slot. Store header (icon, ClipBind PDF, live timestamp, v1.0.0), the headline at display scale, four time-stamped image line items under "Win+V clipboard history: 4 items", the TOTAL = 1 PDF line in red at the largest size, then the red download action at the tear-off. A small JA/EN switch and GitHub link sit outside the receipt.

FORM: Konbini Receipt, position 5 of the ordered grounded list; seed: 65268ca6015a116b. Raises: single red spot (Mid-century Magazine), TOTAL outranks everything (Illuminated Initial), one timeline drives motion: the receipt prints line by line (Paper Automata), honest counters as chrome (Minihompy Room). Signature interaction: the app screenshot is printed in thermal grayscale and develops into full colour on hover/focus/tap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
